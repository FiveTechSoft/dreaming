# Chapter 1: What is TinyLlama? Why TinyLlama?

## The story of TinyLlama

TinyLlama is a small but powerful language model.
It was trained with 1.13 billion parameters distributed
across 22 transformer layers.

## The team behind the model

The TinyLlama team created a model that demonstrates
that you don't need billions of parameters to
produce interesting results.

## Why it is special

TinyLlama is special because:

1. **Manageable size**: With 1.1B parameters, we can
   study its entire structure without getting lost in complexity.

2. **Interesting results**: Despite being small,
   it produces coherent and useful text.

3. **Transparency**: We can visualize and understand
   every component of the model.

## Our motivation for studying it

We study TinyLlama because it allows us to:

- Understand how language models work
- Explore the geometry of the meaning space
- Discover how weight modifications
  can change perspectives
- Learn about the internal structure of transformers

## Conclusion

TinyLlama is a perfect microcosmos for studying
artificial intelligence. In the following chapters,
we will explore its internal structure and discover
its deepest secrets.
