# Chapter 14: The Intermediate Layers (6–12)

## The "pure idea layers"

In the project's reading, the middle stretch of the stack
is where the residual becomes more **abstract**:
themes, roles, frameworks (philosophical, narrative, technical)
rather than just "the next word."

```
Layers 6–12: concepts, broad context, ideas
```

## Why they matter for perspectives

`mystical`-type perturbations act on **all**
attn/FFN layers. The existential climate we saw
in prompts like *When we dissolve the ego* or
*The soul remembers* is compatible with a residual
already "thematized" in the middle of the tunnel — not just
a cosmetic touch-up at the end.

## Semantic islands and the middle of the stack

Embedding directions (emotion, spirit, mind…)
don't "end" at layer 0. The residual **remixes** them.
The intermediate stretch is the candidate for where
`--steer soul` stops being a cosmetic push
and becomes a stable thematic bias.

## Suggested experiment

Project activations (if the engine is instrumented)
from layers 6–12 onto spiritual / abstract / mind
centroids and compare baseline vs mystical.
